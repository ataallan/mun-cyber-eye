Pick a registered authorized camera, run all enabled cameras sequentially, or keep the synthetic MOCK / Phase 3 activity demo. Webcam capture is refused unless ALLOW_WEBCAM=1. Offline or unconfigured cameras are reported honestly — no fake detections.

The activity model does not “know most sports” or “understand all arenas.” Named sport_context and place_type are catalog + folder / camera / color-geometry assists. Kit similarity is a clothing-color cluster, not identity or guilt. Body-aggression cues are OpenCV motion proxies, not a determination of assault.
