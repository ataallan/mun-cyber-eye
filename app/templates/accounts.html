Accounts

New public registrations stay pending until a site admin or Mun Cyber developer approves them. Unapproved accounts cannot sign in, run cameras, or receive alert mail — and they are never sent a sign-in code. After approval, customer admin and operator accounts must enter a one-time code sent to their login email before the console opens. This is notify-only — humans verify and decide.

Your own assignments: My cameras. Operators can review alerts after approval; they cannot approve accounts or train models.
